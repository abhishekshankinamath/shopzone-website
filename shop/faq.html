❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about ShopZone.


How do I place an order?

Browse products, add them to your cart and click "Place Order" on the checkout page.


How can I track my order?

Open the Orders page and click the Track button to see the latest delivery status.


What payment methods are available?

We accept UPI, Debit Cards, Credit Cards, Net Banking and Cash on Delivery.


How do I return a product?

Go to Orders, select the order and click "Return Product".


Can I cancel my order?

Yes, orders can be cancelled before they are shipped.


How do I contact customer support?

Email us at support@shopzone.com or call +91 9876543210.


Do you offer free delivery?

Yes, free delivery is available on selected products.


How do I add items to my wishlist?

Click the ❤ Wishlist button on any product card.
